How it works

1

Discover

Browses trending searches (festivals, seasons, evergreen picks) and filters by rating and price.

2

Convert

Turns each product into your EarnKaro affiliate link automatically.

3

Write

Generates titles and descriptions with a built-in affiliate disclosure.

4

Post

Schedules 6 pins a day to the matching Pinterest boards.

Features

  • Local dashboard to review, edit and skip pins
  • Dry-run mode so nothing posts until you're ready
  • Impressions, saves and click tracking
  • Monthly goals and earnings tracker
  • Trend-based search refresh for the India festival calendar
  • Retries with backoff; runs on your own laptop or Docker

Get started

python -m venv .venv
pip install -r requirements.txt
playwright install chromium
uvicorn app.main:app --port 8000

Then open http://localhost:8000 and click Connect Pinterest.
